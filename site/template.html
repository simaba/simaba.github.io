04 / ABOUT

About the
practice.

My background combines mechatronics, engineering quality and technical program management. The work I publish here moves between program artifacts, executable utilities, process analysis and evaluation methods. Those are different kinds of outputs, and I label them accordingly.

I work on technical questions at both the system and delivery levels: requirements, evidence, handoffs, testing, authority and operational improvement. These public projects are independent work with fictional or synthetic examples; they do not represent employers or clients.

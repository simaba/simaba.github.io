04 / ABOUT

About the
practice.

My work draws on technical program management, engineering quality and applied AI evaluation. I am particularly interested in the decisions surrounding systems: what to test, what evidence to retain, and when to stop, change or release.

These are independent projects, not employer products. Some contain runnable software; others are methods, templates or references. The descriptions distinguish those forms, and their READMEs explain what the tools do not establish.
